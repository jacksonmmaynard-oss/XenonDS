# XenonDS v0.9.1

v0.9.1 is an experimental Xbox 360 homebrew prerelease built around the NooDS
interpreter core. It keeps v0.9.0's completed-frame timing fix and adds a safer,
more honest Turbo path plus measured scheduler and presentation improvements.

## Highlights

- Fixes the white-screen and 0-FPS regression caused by pacing internal ARM
  scheduler returns as if they were completed Nintendo DS frames.
- Adds Normal and uncapped Turbo modes. Turbo presents one complete frame in
  four while continuing DS CPU, timer, input, capture, and game logic work.
- Reports `EMU`, `VID`, and actual DS-clock `SPEED` separately.
- Reserves Xbox hardware context 5 for video and contexts 2/3/4 for software 3D.
- Adds faster Xenon scheduler dispatch and an optimized RGB555 presenter.
- Preserves persistent saves and live brightness, contrast, and color settings.
- Fully restores both DS screens after the picture menu closes.

Turbo reduces the number of television updates by design. Judge its effect with
`EMU` and `SPEED`, not `VID` alone. It is uncapped, but it is not advertised as
a guaranteed 2x or 4x multiplier. Performance still varies by game and scene.

## Install or upgrade

1. Back up `XenonDS/game.sav` if you already have one.
2. Extract `XenonDS-v0.9.1-USB.zip` to the root of a FAT32 USB drive.
3. Keep the loader at `/xenon.elf`.
4. Keep the emulator core at `/XenonDS/xenonds-core.elf32`.
5. Add your legally dumped ROM as `/XenonDS/game.nds`.
6. Boot XeLL and press **A** at the XenonDS loader prompt.

When upgrading from v0.9.0, replace `xenon.elf` and
`XenonDS/xenonds-core.elf32`. Keep your ROM, `.sav`, and display configuration.

## Controls

- **D-pad / left stick:** Nintendo DS D-pad
- **A/B/X/Y:** Nintendo DS face buttons
- **LB/RB:** Nintendo DS L/R
- **LT:** toggle Normal and Turbo
- **Right stick:** touchscreen cursor
- **RT:** touchscreen press
- **R3:** open or close picture settings
- **D-pad in picture settings:** select and adjust
- **A in picture settings:** reset picture settings
- **B or R3 in picture settings:** save and close

## Validation

- Host build and tests
- Full NooDS core, frontend, and video suite
- 36 Turbo and display-capture combinations
- 2,000-frame cadence stress test with maximum handoff gap 3
- 602 legitimate all-white frames
- 100 picture-menu open and close cycles
- Exact framebuffer, capture, and renderer hashes
- Big-endian PowerPC ELF32 build and embedded v0.9.1 version checks
- Runtime checksums and corresponding-source reconstruction

## Known limitations

- Audio is not implemented.
- There is no ROM browser; the build loads `XenonDS/game.nds`.
- Performance is below full Nintendo DS speed in demanding scenes.
- Compatibility testing is currently limited.
- A PowerPC JIT is still required for a large CPU-performance increase.

Use only ROMs, BIOS/firmware, and other material legally dumped from hardware
and games you own. No proprietary Nintendo or Microsoft files are included.
