XenonDS v0.9.1 validated Turbo build
Core: NooDS
NooDS upstream commit: 4cb1439c330248b26cdf5f8dbcfa4e5ac53dcda0

Pacing, statistics, and save cadence belong exclusively to exact NooDS
end-of-frame events. CPU halt/resume scheduler returns are never delayed or
counted as DS frames, preventing the white-screen/0-FPS timing regression.
Normal mode renders every frame. Left-trigger Turbo is uncapped and hands off
one complete rendered frame in four while DS CPUs, input, timers, and game
logic continue every frame. Capture-based video and effects still receive
fresh internal 2D/3D pixels on skipped television frames.

Presentation exclusively owns hardware context 5; three software-3D workers
use contexts 2/3/4. The presenter uses a verified RGB555 handoff, faster Xenos
tile addressing, and changed-region cache flushes. Xenon scheduler events use
direct task dispatch instead of std::function indirection. Matched host A/B
tests measured roughly 3% improvement on mixed ARM work and 10% on
branch/event-heavy work; real-console results remain game dependent.

The build reports completed emulated frames (EMU), television handoffs (VID),
and actual DS-clock speed separately. It restores the entire framebuffer after
repeated display-menu open/close cycles and includes direct boot, dual-screen
video, controller/touch input, persistent display controls, and periodic plus
exit-time cartridge-save flushing. Audio is disabled.
