XenonDS v0.8.0 multicore 3D preview
Core: NooDS
NooDS upstream commit: 4cb1439c330248b26cdf5f8dbcfa4e5ac53dcda0

This build uses direct boot, dual-screen video, controller/touch input, a live
30-frame FPS counter, and periodic plus exit-time cartridge-save flushing. Its
Xbox-specific interpreter/event hot path is LTO-optimized. NooDS software 3D
uses four worker hardware contexts with scanline polygon bins. Audio is disabled.
