XenonDS v0.8.1 color and pacing preview
Core: NooDS
NooDS upstream commit: 4cb1439c330248b26cdf5f8dbcfa4e5ac53dcda0

This build uses direct boot, dual-screen video, controller/touch input, separate
EMU/VID counters, one-frame performance skipping, DS-oriented color correction,
and periodic plus exit-time cartridge-save flushing. Its Xbox-specific
interpreter/event hot path is LTO-optimized. NooDS software 3D uses four worker
hardware contexts with scanline polygon bins. Audio is disabled.
